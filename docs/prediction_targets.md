# NEXORA AI

## Potential Machine Learning Prediction Targets

**Prepared By:** Yashvant Thakur

## 1. Introduction

The objective of Nexora AI is to explore how artificial intelligence and machine learning can help retail businesses make better decisions using their data.

The following are potential machine learning prediction targets.

## 2. Sales Forecasting

Objective:

Predict future sales using historical sales information.

Examples:

* Predict next week's sales.
* Predict next month's revenue.
* Identify future sales trends.

Possible ML techniques:

* Time Series Forecasting.
* Regression.

## 3. Demand Forecasting

Objective:

Predict future customer demand for different products.

Examples:

* Estimate the number of units required next month.
* Identify products with increasing demand.
* Estimate product demand during seasonal periods.

Possible ML techniques:

* Regression.
* Time Series Forecasting.

## 4. Customer Segmentation

Objective:

Group customers based on their purchasing behavior.

Examples:

* Frequent customers.
* Occasional customers.
* High-value customers.

Possible ML techniques:

* K-Means Clustering.
* Hierarchical Clustering.

## 5. Inventory Prediction

Objective:

Predict future inventory requirements and identify potential stock shortages.

Examples:

* Identify products that may run out of stock.
* Estimate restocking requirements.
* Identify low-stock products.

Possible ML techniques:

* Regression.
* Time Series Forecasting.

## 6. Customer Churn Prediction

Objective:

Identify customers who may stop purchasing from the business.

Examples:

* Identify inactive customers.
* Detect decreasing purchasing activity.
* Identify customers at risk of leaving.

Possible ML techniques:

* Logistic Regression.
* Decision Trees.
* Random Forest.

## 7. Product Recommendation

Objective:

Recommend relevant products based on customer purchasing behavior.

Examples:

* Recommend related products.
* Identify frequently purchased product combinations.
* Suggest products based on previous purchases.

Possible ML techniques:

* Collaborative Filtering.
* Content-Based Filtering.
* Recommendation Systems.

## 8. Initial Project Priorities

The following prediction targets can be investigated first:

1. Sales Forecasting.
2. Demand Forecasting.
3. Inventory Prediction.

These targets are initial suggestions and will be finalized based on dataset availability, business requirements, and data quality.

## 9. Conclusion

Machine learning can help retail businesses understand customer behavior, forecast sales, manage inventory, and improve business decisions.

The Nexora AI team will evaluate these prediction opportunities after understanding the actual dataset.
